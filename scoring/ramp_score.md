# Ramp Score

New product ramp difficulty must include:

- `level`: low, medium, high, or very_high
- `ramp_reason`
- `review_threshold`
- `low_review_high_sales_count`
- `ad_dependency`
- `brand_concentration`
- `organic_ranking_barrier`
- `rating_requirement`
- `variation_complexity`

High brand concentration, high review moat, high ad dependency, poor rating tolerance, and complex variation structure increase ramp difficulty.
