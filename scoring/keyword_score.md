# Keyword Score

Classify keywords into:

- core big terms
- product terms
- attribute terms
- audience terms
- scenario terms
- material terms
- seasonal terms
- problem terms
- long-tail conversion terms
- competitor title terms

Score keyword entry with search volume, trend, CPC proxy, search result density, organic ranking barrier, relevance, and conversion intent. Missing trend or CPC lowers confidence, not the factual keyword list.
