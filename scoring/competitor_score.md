# Competitor Score

Use this when selecting comparable products for competitor structure and ramp difficulty.

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Relevance | 35% |
| Category consistency | 20% |
| Price band consistency | 15% |
| Review quality | 10% |
| Rating stability | 10% |
| Style / material / audience match | 10% |

Reject or down-weight competitors that are wrong category, wrong audience, non-comparable bundle, materially different price band, or obviously irrelevant to the entry idea.
